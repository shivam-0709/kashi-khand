About Varanasi

Varanasi, the sacred city of Kashi, is the beating heart of India’s spiritual soul. Known as the gateway to Moksha, it glows with saffron skies and the eternal flames of devotion. The Ganga flows here not just as a river, but as a living goddess, carrying centuries of prayers and hopes. From the chants of dawn to the shimmering lamps of Ganga Aarti, Varanasi is a timeless journey into faith, culture, and enlightenment.

Temples

Visit the divine Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, and Durga Kund. Each shrine radiates centuries of devotion and spiritual energy.

Ghats & Boat Rides

Experience the mystical ghats like Dashashwamedh and Assi. A boat ride on the Ganga at sunrise reveals the city’s eternal beauty.

Festivals

Celebrate Dev Deepawali, Ganga Mahotsav, and Holi in the streets of Kashi. The city bursts into colors, lights, and chants of joy.

Shopping Highlights

Take home Banarasi sarees, brass idols, wooden toys, and the famous Banarasi paan. The bazaars are a treasure chest of tradition.
